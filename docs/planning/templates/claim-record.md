# Bounded Scientific Claim Template

Create in the D09 claim register when a statement is ready for review.

## Claim identity and scope

Claim ID/version/date; exact statement; status PROPOSED / UNDER-REVIEW / SUPPORTED-IN-DOMAIN / LIMITED / REFUTED-IN-DOMAIN / WITHDRAWN; parent hypothesis/task.

Domain: body/material/size and concentration; medium/state; geometry/boundaries; source/frequency/actuation; model/solver/precision; frame/gravity environment; initial states; duration/observation; parameter/uncertainty set.

Quantifiers: one/all cases, one/all admissible controls, nominal/robust, local/global; target observable and threshold.

## Warranted interpretation

The smallest statement justified by all required evidence, what remains unknown, and what new evidence could change it. Distinguish failure of software/model/controller from failure of the physical target. No body-independent, human-scale or actual microgravity claim follows from an unmatched simulation.

## Review history

Reviewer/date, actual decision, prior wording retained, reason for revision, affected experiment/plan updates and next investigation.

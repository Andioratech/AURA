# AURA-D09: Scientific Register, Claims and Literature

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** DRAFT · **Date:** 2026-10-01

## Claim states

Use PROPOSED, UNDER-REVIEW, SUPPORTED-IN-DOMAIN, LIMITED, REFUTED-IN-DOMAIN and WITHDRAWN. State the domain and evidence for every disposition. A simulation result cannot be promoted directly to a general physical claim.

## Claim record

Each claim has a stable ID, exact statement, scope/domain, assumptions, measurable prediction, supporting and contradicting runs, MCLF verdicts, benchmark/experimental links, uncertainty, reviewer, status and revision history. Claims about universal gravity, human suitability or scale-up require evidence beyond the initial computational scope.

## Equation register

For every equation record symbol definitions and units, source and page/section, derivation or assumptions, validity domain, implementation locations, independent check, related tests and version. Distinguish primary literature from secondary summaries. Verify bibliographic metadata and equation interpretation before relying on a citation.

The source PDFs suggest the following starting points. Metadata has been checked against publisher, journal or scholarly index records; equation applicability still requires full-text review:

Gor'kov, “Forces acting on a small particle in an acoustic field within an ideal fluid,” Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR, 140(1), 88-91 (1961); English translation cited as Soviet Physics Doklady 6, 773 (1962). The source proposal's title/year pairing should be normalized when a specific edition is cited.
- H. Bruus, “Acoustofluidics 7: The acoustic radiation force on small particles,” Lab on a Chip 12, 1014-1021 (2012), DOI 10.1039/C2LC21068A.
- M. Settnes and H. Bruus, “Forces acting on a small particle in an acoustical field in a viscous fluid,” Physical Review E 85, 016327 (2012), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.85.016327.
- A. Marzo et al., “Holographic acoustic elements for manipulation of levitated objects,” Nature Communications 6, 8661 (2015), DOI 10.1038/ncomms9661.

These sources address small-particle radiation force or levitated-object manipulation in specific configurations; none alone validates AURA's proposed extended-body acceleration or scale-up. Add authoritative sources for acoustic momentum/stress formulations, numerical methods and safety as the relevant models are chosen.

## Novelty and publication

Maintain a dated novelty log with search terms, databases, closest prior work and reviewer. Separate demonstrated outcomes from proposed designs. Publication language must identify model assumptions, finite tested domain, uncertainty, failed cases and whether evidence is simulated or measured. Patent/publication decisions require project-owner review; this document makes no IP claim.

## Delivered intermediate component

The first coupled implementation is now present in `src/aura/fields/hasegawa.py`. It combines the scaled Hasegawa piston source factors with the stationary sound-hard sphere branch at every order, including `n=1`, and returns FIELD-1.0 peak complex pressure, particle velocity and pressure gradient for caller-supplied exterior Cartesian points. It is restricted to `kr <= 40`, orders 0–512, a coaxial piston at `z=0`, and a stationary sphere centered at the declared positive-z distance. Points on/behind the piston-baffle plane or inside the sphere are rejected. A conservative local workspace gate executes before special-function/modal arrays; it is not yet integrated with NUM-02's run-level preflight or runtime calibration. The caller supplies piston face velocity (the frozen 15 µm displacement must be converted using the declared phasor convention) and explicit order; this function never selects an order or labels a result converged.

The added coupled checks use a separate unscaled modal calculation at a common off-axis exterior point for pressure, velocity and pressure gradient; verify zero normal velocity at the front and rear poles and a 60° off-axis sphere point; exercise the order-sequence diagnostic; and check rejection before source-array construction. At the 0.1 mm gap poles, order 280→300 pressure changes are `2.27e-13` (front) and `1.59e-11` (rear). These are two-pole diagnostics, not full-surface convergence evidence or production tolerances. Eight focused coupled checks pass; the final full Quality count will be recorded after the documentation/review update.

# SC-02 — Establish the Required Model Change

**Status:** DONE as bounded literature/model-contract research · **Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Responsible role:** research implementer · **Baseline:** D00 v1.3 / PLAN-01 v1.3 · **Code revision:** `9ee66f7b1fb79eff807c078c102aae17bab09db6`

## Question and scope

Determine whether the model used for small spherical particles can be carried into the large, non-spherical candidate regime identified in SC-01. Review primary source assumptions and propose a bounded verification route without implementing a solver.

## Method and output

Prepared [scale-model-change.md](../research/scale-model-change.md) from the SC-01 dossier, LIT-03 model review, D02/D04/D06 contracts, Inoue et al.'s open primary manuscript and independent partial-wave sphere references. Inspected the source equations for boundary scattering, force/torque integrals, rigid-body linearization and stability, plus its experimental limitations.

## Finding

The particle-scale sphere equations cannot be extrapolated to an approximately 50-mm non-spherical body at about 5.9 acoustic wavelengths. Body-induced scattering and orientation-dependent torque require a surface-resolved rigid-body model. Boundary elements are a literature-supported candidate, but the cited study's sound-hard, inviscid air assumptions and measured force discrepancy prevent promotion to an AURA-validated model. No valid AURA boundary/material conditions, field, target or actuator set are frozen.

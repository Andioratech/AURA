# SC-01 — Register Scale and Geometry Candidates

**Status:** DONE as a bounded literature/design task · **Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Responsible role:** research implementer · **Baseline:** D00 v1.3 / PLAN-01 v1.3 · **Code revision:** `9ee66f7b1fb79eff807c078c102aae17bab09db6`

## Question and scope

Identify source-backed greater-mass, larger-dimension and geometry candidates while preserving DEC-004. This task selects literature candidates for follow-on analysis, not an AURA hardware target or physical experiment.

## Method and output

Prepared [scale-candidates.md](../research/scale-candidates.md) from the existing P1.1 air benchmark and primary reports by Andrade et al. (2016), Inoue et al. (2019) and Oran et al. (1979). Recorded the specimens, medium, frequency/wavelength where available, field/array arrangement, reported observables, co-varying parameters, missing uncertainty/calibration and independent-check route. The comparison separates the 50-mm, 1.46-g EPS sphere force reference from the 50-mm-diagonal, approximately 0.5-g EPS octahedron rigid-body case.

## Findings

- Published air experiments cover bodies larger in mass and dimension than AURA's initial microparticle water domain; this does not establish transfer to water or any AURA target.
- The available sources do not isolate mass as a single changed variable. Medium, frequency, body material, shape, array and boundary conditions also differ.
- Inoue et al. reports a body dimension about 5.9 wavelengths and studies restoring force and estimated torque; it supports investigating a new rigid-body model, not reusing a small-particle law. Reported static levitation is not prescribed acceleration.
- Prior measurements and uncertainty limits remain intact. No AURA feasibility, novelty, gravity-equivalence or performance claim follows.

## Acceptance and validation

At least one greater-mass candidate and one larger-dimension/non-spherical candidate have exact primary-source references and an explicit comparison of changed/held quantities, assumptions, gaps and independent checks. This is evidence inventory and design work; no code, physical run or solver was used.

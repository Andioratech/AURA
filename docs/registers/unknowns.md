## Retention and updates

Resolve an entry by adding its evidence and exact source/run identity, uncertainty, affected domain and reviewer decision; do not delete its history. If evidence remains unavailable after the research cap, mark the affected comparison INDETERMINATE and continue unrelated eligible work under DEC-003. Changes to the accepted scope, physical target or gate sequence require the owner decision and change-control route in PLAN-01.

## Status interpretation

- **Verified software:** implementation matches a declared independent mathematical/software reference under a frozen criterion.
- **Model verified within scope:** analytical or numerical model behavior agrees with a reference for its exact domain and assumptions.
- **Measurement comparison:** outputs are compared with a matched measured observable and its calibration/uncertainty.
- **Experimental validation:** requires the applicable matched measurement, uncertainty and predeclared decision rule; a plotted curve without measurement uncertainty cannot produce a formal PASS here.
- **Planned or unsupported:** a plan, source lead, interface, or requirement does not demonstrate implementation or scientific capability.

The current records support bounded software verification for foundations and ideal fields. They do not establish that acoustic pseudogravity creates gravity, that AURA can accelerate a physical body, or that any model scales to a different body, geometry, medium or regime. Physical validation remains NOT ESTABLISHED. See [D09](../D09-scientific-register.md), [DEC-003](../decisions/DEC-003-nonblocking-foundation-work.md) and [DEC-004](../decisions/DEC-004-staged-mass-and-size-expansion.md).

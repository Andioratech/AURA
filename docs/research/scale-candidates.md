# Scale and Geometry Campaign Candidates

**Prepared:** 2026-10-03 · **Work item:** SC-01 · **Status:** literature-backed candidate screen; no target or hardware selected

## Purpose and scope

This screen implements DEC-004's direction to preserve research into greater masses, larger dimensions and other geometries after the small-particle water starting campaign. It identifies reported bodies and useful evidence without treating any of them as an approved AURA target. Values below describe published apparatus and specimens, not AURA requirements, scale milestones or feasibility thresholds.

## What the comparison does and does not isolate

The available studies establish that published air experiments have levitated bodies much larger than the initial water microparticles and have included an aspherical rigid body. They do not provide a clean mass-only ladder: body material, medium, wavelength, geometry, array and field conditions also change. Nor do they establish a direct conversion from levitation force to a sustained target acceleration. In a proposed comparison, record mass, dimensions, shape, density/compressibility, medium, wavelength, source geometry, boundary conditions and target separately; label every confounded change.

The geometry transition in SC-C02 is especially relevant because its reported diagonal is about `50/8.5 ≈ 5.9` wavelengths. This is far outside the small-particle limit used for SRC-W01/SRC-W02. The ratio is a descriptive calculation from the paper's geometry and wavelength, not a substitute force-model applicability analysis. SC-02 must select and independently check an appropriate rigid-body model before any numerical force or trajectory test.

## Recommendation and gates

Prioritize SC-C02 for the next *model-applicability research question*, because the aspherical body and dimension-above-wavelength regime expose two explicit model changes (shape-resolved field interaction and torque) and the paper reports an experimentally accessible restoring-force observable. Keep SC-C01 as the better-aligned separate measured-force reference. Neither is selected as an AURA physical target. The owner has recorded the bounded ANA-07/P3 PASS; SC-C02 still requires the relevant P4/P5 field and force-model gates before claim-critical computation. Before a target-acceleration study, separately complete the applicable force, dynamics and target contracts. Do not infer that levitation or local restoring stiffness means spatially uniform acceleration or gravity equivalence.

The candidate search found no source-backed AURA actuator budget, long-duration envelope, calibrated transducer limit or microgravity residual-acceleration series. Those remain unknowns, not zeros. Later searches should match the chosen medium, body and observable; compare rigid-body scattering/torque methods; and retrieve uncertainty and calibration data before setting a threshold. SC-01 makes no feasibility or novelty claim and does not authorize solver construction.

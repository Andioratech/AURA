# DEC-004: Small Particles as the Starting Case, Larger Bodies as a Research Objective

**Status:** RECORDED OWNER DIRECTION · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Direction and interpretation

The owner clarified that AURA must not become a project limited to particle motion. Small particles in water are the initial tractable case. The research objective also includes greater masses, larger objects and other body geometries, with microgravity as the intended long-term environment.

This authorizes including scale progression in the research plan. It does not assert that any proposed scale is achievable or approve unspecified operating parameters, model equations or hardware.

## Implementation in the plan

1. Describe the overall objective in terms of controlled forces and prescribed acceleration for selected bodies. Label the water-particle implementation as the first campaign.
2. Include a required progression investigation for increasing mass, dimensions and geometric complexity; select numerical ranges from evidence rather than inventing gram/kilogram milestones.
3. Research mass, size, material and shape as separate changes where possible. Document coupled changes when they cannot be isolated physically.
4. At every new regime, review model applicability, source/actuator constraints, force and torque, dynamics, uncertainty and matched independent evidence. Reuse software infrastructure only within its documented contracts.
5. Start higher-mass/larger-body literature and applicability research alongside foundations. New numerical campaigns remain behind the relevant P2–P9 gates. A failure of one particle case does not close the overall scale investigation.
6. Preserve all earlier limitations, including the separate air benchmark and its missing experimental uncertainty. No old run is reinterpreted as evidence for a larger body.

## Affected documents and evidence

D00, D01, PLAN-01, the planning objective/board/software map and the new [scale progression plan](../planning/11-scale-progression.md) are clarified. PLAN-01 v1.2 is retained in the archive; v1.3 links the scale track. No accepted equation, tolerance, result or D03 acceptance requirement is changed. Specific new case definitions still require their own review before claim-critical runs.
